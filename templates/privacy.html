Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our personal expense tracking service (the "Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and login credentials when you register an account. We also collect the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. Basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, may be collected automatically to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the Service, including creating and managing your account, displaying your expense history, and generating spending summaries. We may also use your data to communicate with you about your account, respond to support requests, and improve the features and reliability of Spendly. We do not sell your personal or financial data.

3. Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party providers for functions such as hosting, analytics, or email delivery. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their functions and are expected to handle it in accordance with applicable privacy standards. We do not share your expense data with third parties for advertising purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through your account settings.
